Build a daily Japanese study habit with Pengo, an offline companion for JLPT N5–N1.

• Explore kanji meanings, readings, components and animated stroke order.
• Study vocabulary and grammar with example sentences and furigana where available.
• Reveal learning cards and practice with short quizzes.
• Choose a study plan, revisit mistakes and review items with spaced repetition.
• Track your learning activity and continue saved sessions.
• Set optional daily reminders that work while the app is closed.
• Choose a light or dark theme and export or import progress backups.

Study content is included in the app. No account, advertising, analytics or internet connection is required. Progress is stored on your device; export a backup before uninstalling or moving to another device.

Dictionary, sentence and stroke-order credits are available in Settings → Credits & data sources.
